## 1. COMPETITOR COMPARISON

### Arthritis UK (arthritisuk.org.uk)
**Strengths:**
- Established UK charity (national reach)
- High domain authority (estimated DA 70+)
- Comprehensive medical resource library
- Multiple content types (guides, videos, support groups)
- Strong brand recognition
- Estimated 500K+ monthly organic visitors
- Estimated 5,000+ ranking keywords
- Robust internal linking structure
- Heavy investment in SEO infrastructure

**Content Gaps & Weaknesses:**
- Limited sexual health/intimacy coverage (1-2 articles)
- Minimal caregiver support content (scattered, not pillar-focused)
- Generic mental health approach (not arthritis-specific)
- No "Access to Work" scheme deep-dive (UK-specific advantage missed)
- Limited fatigue management strategies
- Formulaic article approach (less personality/user empathy)

### Living with Arthritis (livingwitharthritis.org.uk)
**Current Strengths:**
- Smaller, focused scope (easier to dominate niches)
- 248 blog articles (good foundation)
- Solopreneur operation (agile, responsive)
- Optimized technical SEO (post-audit fixes)
- Clear internal structure

**Current Weaknesses:**
- Lower domain authority (estimated DA 20-30)
- Limited brand awareness vs Arthritis UK
- Fewer ranking keywords (estimated 200-300)
- 192 orphan pages (content not connected)
- 69 long page titles (CTR impact)
- Missing high-ROI niches (sexual health, caregiver support)
- No featured snippet strategy

   - 4 detailed articles on pacing, sleep, exercise, energy management
