- A3/A4-friendly portrait ratio (print + social sharing).

## Steps
1. Screenshot/review the landing page hero for exact brand cues (fonts, red tone, logo).
2. Generate the poster artwork as a PNG via the image tool, guided by those cues.
3. Produce a clean, high-resolution final poster PNG (portrait, ~1240×1754) saved to /mnt/documents as a downloadable file, plus a PDF version for printing.
4. Visually inspect the output (no clipped text, nothing overlapping, details legible) and fix if needed.
