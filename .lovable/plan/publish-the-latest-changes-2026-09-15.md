# Publish the latest changes

Push the current version of the site live to livingwitharthritis.org.uk.

## What goes live
- The corrected share buttons on blog posts (Facebook, LinkedIn and email now share the article itself)
- The updated UK benefits guide summary (PIP, Adult Disability Payment, ESA, Universal Credit, Blue Badge)
- The agent integrations that let assistants search and read your published guides

## Steps
1. Run a security check and report anything critical before publishing.
2. Publish the site.
3. Confirm the live address responds once the deployment finishes (about a minute).

## Notes
No content, layout or navigation changes are made as part of publishing.
