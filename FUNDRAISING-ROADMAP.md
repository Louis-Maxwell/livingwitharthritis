# Fundraising growth roadmap — Living With Arthritis UK
### An honest plan (what actually moves institutional money)

**Reality check first.** Versus Arthritis (est. 1936, national brand) raises ~£25M/yr;
Arthritis Foundation (US) ~$70M. A £140M year is not achievable for any young charity
through website changes — anyone who promises it is selling snake oil. What follows is
the real ladder. Each rung is what corporates, councils and grant-makers actually check.

## Phase 1 — Fundable (this quarter, ~£0 cost)
- Complete the Trust & Governance page (fill the 9 TODOs) — every grant officer looks for it.
- Register with the **Fundraising Regulator** (~£50/yr) and display the badge.
- Confirm **HMRC Gift Aid** recognition; enable Gift Aid on the donate flow (+25% on every eligible gift).
- Publish a 1-page **Impact Report** (people helped, sessions delivered, cost per outcome).
- File on time with the Charity Commission — funders check the register first.

## Phase 2 — Grant income (3–12 months, realistic £20k–£150k/yr)
- **National Lottery Awards for All**: £300–£20k, rolling, high acceptance for health/community.
- **Local authority public-health / social-prescribing contracts**: councils commission falls-prevention
  and MSK self-management — your exercise + frailty content is exactly this. Pitch to your ICB/ICS.
- **Trusts & foundations**: Garfield Weston, Henry Smith, Tudor Trust — health & wellbeing streams.
- **Sport England / Active Partnerships**: strength & balance and tai chi programmes qualify.

## Phase 3 — Corporate & tech (6–18 months, realistic £10k–£100k/yr to start)
- **Tech in-kind first, cash later**: Google Ad Grants ($10k/month free search ads — apply now,
  charities qualify), Microsoft/Google/AWS nonprofit credits, Benevity/Percent listing so
  employees at big firms can give with employer matching.
- **CSR partnerships**: pharma (with strict ABPI transparency), insurers, employers with ageing
  workforces — offer branded MSK-health workshops, not just logo placement.
- **Payroll giving + charity-of-the-year** pitches once the impact report exists.

## Phase 4 — Scale (2–5 years)
- NHS commissioned services, NIHR research partnership income, legacy-giving programme,
  major-donor cultivation. £1M/yr is an excellent 3–5 year target; £5–10M/yr is top-decile.

## What the website's job is in all of this
Not to raise £140M — to never lose a donor who checks you out. That means: verifiable
registration (done), governance (page ready), clinical review credentials (in place),
transparent impact numbers (Phase 1), and a frictionless Gift-Aided donate flow.
