All diagram explanations

Bones
The thigh bone (femur) and shin bone (tibia) meet at the knee. This simplified drawing does not show every bone or tissue.
Cartilage
Cartilage covers the ends of bones at a joint and helps them move with less friction. Osteoarthritis can affect cartilage and other joint tissues.
Other joint tissues
The knee also has menisci, ligaments, tendons and a joint lining. Osteoarthritis is a whole-joint condition, not simply bones wearing out.
