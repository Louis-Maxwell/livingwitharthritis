# Traffic Diversion Strategy
## How to Capture Arthritis Search Traffic to Living With Arthritis UK

**Goal:** Become the #1 destination for UK arthritis information, support, and resources.

**Current State:**
- 20k organic social followers (Facebook + Instagram)
- Strong content foundation (100+ blog posts)
- Independent charity positioning
- HCPC-registered physiotherapist founder
- Free resources (exercises, nutrition, community)

**Target:** 10x monthly traffic within 12 months (50k → 500k+ monthly visits)

---

## **TIER 1: Quick Wins (Weeks 1-4) — Immediate Traffic Capture**

### 1.1 High-Intent Keyword Targeting
**Strategy:** Rank for searches where people are ready to convert (get help, donate, join)

**High-Intent Keywords to Target First:**

```
"arthritis physio exercises uk"
"arthritis support group uk"
"arthritis community online"
"free arthritis exercises"
"arthritis exercise routine"
"how to manage arthritis pain"
"arthritis diet uk"
"arthritis benefits pip uk"
"arthritis flare-up management"
"arthritis physiotherapy free"
```

**Action:**
1. Audit current rankings: `npm run seo:inventory`
2. For keywords where you rank #4-10: Add internal links + refresh content
3. For keywords where you rank #11+: Create targeted landing page
4. For keywords not covered: Create new blog post (2-3 per week)

**Expected gain:** 500-1000 monthly visits in 4 weeks

---

### 1.2 Optimize Existing High-Volume Pages
**Goal:** Squeeze more traffic from pages already ranking

**Pages to prioritize (check your rank tracking):**
- Homepage (`/`) — optimize for "arthritis support uk"
- Blog index (`/blog`) — optimize for "arthritis blog"
- Conditions pages (Osteoarthritis, Rheumatoid Arthritis, Gout, etc.) — already strong
- Guides (exercises, diet) — high-intent pages

**Action per page:**
1. Analyze current rank + CTR in Google Search Console
2. Improve meta title/description for click-through
3. Add FAQ section (Google shows in "People also ask")
4. Add internal links to related pages
5. Ensure page loads in <2 seconds (Core Web Vitals)

**Expected gain:** 20-30% CTR improvement = +2000-3000 visits/month

"Having [symptom]? Our free [resource] helps [outcome].
Link in bio → [specific page, not just homepage]"

**Expected gain:** 1000-2000 visits/month (if doing ads)

## **TIER 4: Performance Marketing (Optional, If Budget) — Paid Traffic**

### 4.1 Google Ads (Search)
**Strategy:** Bid on competitor keywords + high-intent terms

**Keywords to target:**
- `arthritis support` (£0.50-1.00/click) → broad
- `arthritis exercises` (£0.30-0.60/click) → high intent
- `rheumatoid arthritis uk` (£0.40-0.80/click) → specific
- `arthritis physio` (£0.20-0.50/click) → very high intent
- Brand keywords (Arthritis UK competitors) (£0.10-0.30/click) → cheap

**Budget allocation:**
- If you have £500/month: Focus on high-intent only (exercises, physio, support)
- If you have £2000/month: Add competitor keywords
- If you have £5000+/month: Scale across all keywords

**Expected ROI:** 
- High-intent keywords: 10-15% donation conversion
- Competitor keywords: 3-5% sign-ups
- Total: £1 spend → £2-3 value (if optimized well)

---

### 4.2 Facebook/Instagram Ads
**Strategy:** Convert your social audience into email subscribers

**Ad types:**
- Lead generation ads: "Free Arthritis Guide" → email capture
- Video ads: 15-30s exercise snippets → website
- Carousel ads: 5 arthritis myths → myth-busting pages
- Collection ads: "Browse 100+ arthritis guides"

**Targeting:**
- Interests: Arthritis, rheumatology, fitness, health
- Age: 40-75 (highest arthritis prevalence)
- Location: UK + Ireland
- Lookalike audiences: Your email subscribers

**Budget allocation:**
- If you have £200/month: Lead gen only (capture emails)
- If you have £1000+/month: Mix of traffic + leads

**Expected ROI:** 
- Cost per email: £0.20-0.50
- Email list growth: +1000-2000/month
- Conversion to support/donation: 5-10% of list
