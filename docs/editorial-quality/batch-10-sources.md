## Evidence boundaries
Cochrane2013: six trials/624adults; search to2March2012. GI effects, abnormal transaminases and withdrawal improved; mouth-ulcer reduction not statistically significant; blood-cell evidence insufficient. No prevention-of-all-toxicity claim. NHS and SPS folic-acid schedules differ, so use the patient prescription, not a new dose schedule. NHS permits alcohol in many low-dose cases; Arthritis UK reports stricter individual limits. No personal safe allowance. SPS permits some clinician-monitored NSAID combinations while discouraging unreviewed OTC NSAIDs; prescribed medicines are not stopped from a list alone. NRAS injection option is not a guaranteed nausea cure. NIurgent route separately verified.
