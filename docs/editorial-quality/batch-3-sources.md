## Sources and claim boundaries

- NHS patient information, methotrexate and other medicines/supplements. Page review14March2023, displayed next review due14March2026. Live text checked, cross-checked against newer SPS professional guidance. Insufficient supplement safety evidence; do not add folate products to existing prescribed folic acid without advice. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/methotrexate/taking-methotrexate-with-other-medicines-and-herbal-supplements/
- NHS SPS professional guidance, managing low-dose methotrexate interactions. Updated30June2025. Main clearance is renal, not a claim of shared primary CYP liver clearance. NSAID co-prescribing can be specialist-managed; bought NSAIDs need advice. Trimethoprim/co-trimoxazole severe bone-marrow risk. Not a complete interaction database. https://sps.nhs.uk/articles/managing-interactions-with-methotrexate/
- NHS SPS professional guidance, folic acid with methotrexate,20February2023. Several regimens, usually avoid methotrexate day, reduces side effects. Does not support the former absolute claim that an extra dose stops treatment entirely. No new dose schedule prescribed here. https://sps.nhs.uk/articles/using-folic-acid-with-methotrexate-in-rheumatoid-arthritis/
- UK Committee on Toxicity assessment,9December2024. Reasonable evidence for turmeric-associated liver toxicity, rare unpredictable supplement reactions even below dietary ADI; altered formulations can alter exposure. Does not prove a specific methotrexate interaction mechanism or safe supplement dose. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/turmeric-and-curcumin-supplements/statement-on-the-potential-risk-to-human-health-of-turmeric-and-curcumin-supplements-lay-summary
- Welsh Medicines Advice Service professional review,22October2024. Interaction information mostly in-vitro/animal, may not translate clinically. Caution with immunosuppressants/significant-side-effect medicines; possible bleeding concerns and formulations matter. No methotrexate-specific interval established on this page. https://www.wmic.wales.nhs.uk/turmeric-potential-interactions/
- TGA regulator safety alert,15August2023. Rare liver injury in medicinal forms, possibly greater enhanced-absorption/high-dose risks but no definitive product ranking. Typical food amounts distinguished. Stop supplement/seek advice if liver-warning symptoms. Australian regulator source used for product safety, not UK care pathways. https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/safety-monitoring-and-information/safety-alerts/medicines-containing-turmeric-or-curcumin-risk-liver-injury
- NHS SPS professional guidance, complementary products, updated16July2026. Case-by-case review, full product label, medical context, limited theory/case-report evidence. https://sps.nhs.uk/articles/managing-complementary-products-and-conventional-medicines/
- NHS patient information, sulfasalazine, reviewed6March2025. Herbal safety uncertain, folic-acid absorption can be reduced. No blanket multivitamin two-hour rule established. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/sulfasalazine/taking-sulfasalazine-with-other-medicines-and-herbal-supplements/
- NHS patient information, adalimumab, current page accessed17November2026. Tell care team about supplements; infection/live-vaccine cautions. Does not establish that every immune-support supplement overrides the biologic. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/adalimumab/
- NHS patient information, ibuprofen, current page accessed17November2026. Other NSAID duplication and serious-side-effect risks; existing aspirin should be discussed, not stopped by reader. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/ibuprofen-for-adults/
- NHS patient information, methotrexate side effects, current page accessed17November2026. Doctor/111 immediately for named liver, infection, blood and lung warning signs;999for serious allergy. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/methotrexate/side-effects-of-methotrexate/
