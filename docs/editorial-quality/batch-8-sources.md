# Alcohol and arthritis source ledger, 19 November 2026

## Scope and evidence conflicts
Existing public article selected by risk, not claimed traffic rank. Its lead turned an association into a general treatment-benefit assertion; body contained unrelated generic filler, unsupported pain percentages and response timelines, implied clinical contacts and mixed emergency routing. Replacement separates RA evidence, gout, general UK low-risk guidance, exact medicines and dependence safety. No advice to start alcohol for arthritis, no universal safe drinking allowance, no DIY withdrawal taper, no change to prescribed treatment. Same route, original publication date and author retained, inherited review fields removed; fresh qualified clinical review explicitly pending.

The NRAS page updated 9 April 2025 describes positive RA symptom associations, while EULAR's evidence review describes flare risk. The newer 2023 Swedish cohort also reports a positive association and disputes reverse causation as a full explanation; the earlier longitudinal US study found no clear benefit after accounting for confounding. The revision names both findings and observational limits rather than claiming all positive studies are refuted or presenting a causal benefit. Not a systematic review of all alcohol research or a personalised prescribing opinion.

## Sources read and claims used
- NHS, The risks of drinking too much, reviewed 10 September 2026. National patient guidance: regular weekly 14-unit low-risk ceiling, spread over three or more days if drinking that amount, no risk-free level, pregnancy advice. https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/alcohol-advice/the-risks-of-drinking-too-much/
- NHS, Alcohol units. National patient guidance: ABV percentage x millilitres / 1,000. Examples independently calculated: 175 ml at 12%=2.1 units; 500 ml at 5%=2.5; 25 ml at 40%=1. No driving-clearance arithmetic. https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/alcohol-advice/calculating-alcohol-units/
- NHS, Alcohol-use disorder, reviewed 24 April 2026. National patient guidance: withdrawal danger, medical help before stopping/reducing, severe withdrawal emergency routing, practical support. Warning placed before drink-free-day advice and before practical changes as well as in safety section. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/alcohol-use-disorder/
- NHS Better Health, Drink less. Public health guidance: lower-strength/smaller/soft-drink options, social plans and support, verified Drinkline number 0300 123 1110. https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/drink-less/
- NHS, Alcohol poisoning. Patient triage: suspected poisoning, loss of consciousness, slow/irregular breathing and seizures require 999. No advice to drive or give home detox treatment. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/alcohol-poisoning/
- NICE NG219, recommendations 1.4.1-1.4.2. Clinical guideline: insufficient evidence for a specific diet to prevent flares/lower urate, balanced diet, excessive alcohol may worsen gout. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng219/chapter/Recommendations
- NHS, Gout. National patient guidance: alcohol/dehydration triggers, continued regular urate-lowering treatment, review if symptoms persist. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/gout/
- NHS, Methotrexate common questions, reviewed 14 March 2023 and review due 14 March 2026, still the live national page on source-check date. Its precise low-weekly-dose <=25 mg inflammatory-condition qualification retained; high-dose question referred to doctor. Overdue review date is not silently promoted to a current clinical review. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/methotrexate/common-questions-about-methotrexate/
- NRAS, Alcohol and RA, updated 9 April 2025. Specialist patient charity: methotrexate/leflunomide liver concern, honest drinking history for blood-test interpretation, individual advice. Positive RA symptom association acknowledged alongside conflicting studies, not adopted as treatment advice. https://nras.org.uk/resource/alcohol-and-ra/
- NHS, NSAIDs, reviewed 28 April 2026. Medicine guidance: large alcohol intake increases side effects/stomach irritation; ulcer/bleeding risk and medicine-specific advice. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/nsaids/
- NHS, Codeine. Medicine guidance: best not to drink because side effects can increase, combination medicines distinct; breathing risks. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/codeine/
- NHS, Amitriptyline for pain common questions. Medicine guidance: alcohol can worsen sleepiness, avoid initially until effects known. No blanket prohibition invented. https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/amitriptyline-for-pain/common-questions-about-amitriptyline-for-pain-and-migraine/
- NHS, Septic arthritis, reviewed 3 September 2026. National triage: urgent GP/111 for sudden severe joint pain/swelling/colour change/movement difficulty, fever absent in some. Do not assume gout from drinking history. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/septic-arthritis/
- Wieczorek et al., RMD Open 2022, systematic reviews informing EULAR recommendations. Associations vary by condition, potential RA/gout flare risks, observational/confounding limitations and sparse evidence. https://rmdopen.bmj.com/content/8/1/e002170
- Changes in Alcohol Use and Associations with Disease Activity, Health Status, and Mortality in Rheumatoid Arthritis, longitudinal observational research. No clear benefit after accounting for confounding, possible reverse causation; does not prove no possible effect at every quantity. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6754326/
- Alfredsson et al., Arthritis & Rheumatology, first published 11 January 2023, cohort of 1,228 newly diagnosed RA patients, one-year follow-up. Positive association remained in stable-drinking comparisons; self-report/observational limits, few heavy drinkers. Included to avoid cherry-picking the earlier null finding. https://acrjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/art.42442
