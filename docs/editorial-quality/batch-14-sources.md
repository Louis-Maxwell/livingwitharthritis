## Evidence boundaries
ESE/ES2024R2.1short-course recommendation under3-4weeks is low certainty, not personal stopping clearance. UKprednisoloneSmPC lists short-course exceptions: repeated/recentlong-termcourses,highdoses,other adrenal risk. NICE NG2431.9 uses adult longer-exposure threshold and clinical-team decisions; no single threshold replaces individual history. NICE1.9.8restricts adult cortisol testing to symptomatic slower-taper context, whereas ESE/ES permits monitored taper or morning cortisol near physiological dose. Both presented, no blanket testing claim/threshold/hold interval. Primary endocrine article directly read for symptom overlap,variation and lack of comparative taper-regimen studies; no fabricated trial consensus or fixed recovery promise. NICE stress-care/hospital advice preserved for impaired oral absorption. NHSAddison primary condition not equated with steroid-induced adrenal insufficiency; emergency signs confined to known/at-risk/severelyunwell context. NHSjointinfection advice urgent even without fever,replacing same-week wording. RAbridge/flare treatment separate from longer-term disease control.
