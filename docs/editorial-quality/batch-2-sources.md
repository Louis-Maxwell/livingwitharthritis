# Benefits editorial batch 2: source notes

Checked 17 November 2026. Two published pages: pip-fibromyalgia-denied-reapply and pip-osteoarthritis-both-knees. Source HTML/text is retained in the research workspace. This is editorial fact checking, not individual legal advice, clinical sign-off or an award prediction.

## Claim-to-source map

- GOV.UK PIP eligibility: age 16+, usual new-claim State Pension age limit and exceptions, expected 12-month difficulties, work/savings and location rules. https://www.gov.uk/pip/eligibility
- GOV.UK claim process: England/Wales starting route, supporting information examples, usual one-month disability-form return and ask-for-more-time route. https://www.gov.uk/pip/how-to-claim
- DWP assessment criteria: reliability, reasonable time, variability and frequency, needs rather than diagnosis; distinct physical moving-around and mental-health/cognitive/sensory journey activity. Sections2.1,2.2and activities11/12. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/personal-independence-payment-assessment-guide-for-assessment-providers/pip-assessment-guide-part-2-the-assessment-criteria
- GOV.UK reconsideration: phone, letter or form choices, usual one month from letter date, receipt deadline for written requests, late request with good reason, evidence linked to dispute, evidence costs not reclaimable. https://www.gov.uk/mandatory-reconsideration/how-to-ask-for-mandatory-reconsideration
- GOV.UK appeal: independent tribunal, ordinarily after reconsideration, free, normally one month after getting reconsideration decision, late appeals not guaranteed. Advice signposts, no representation promise. https://www.gov.uk/appeal-benefit-decision
- Citizens Advice: components/activities/points, reliability and decisions. https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/benefits/sick-or-disabled-people-and-carers/pip/help-with-your-claim/how-decisions-are-made/
- Department for Communities NI handbook: separate claim/dispute process, phone/writing/MR2, dates and direct TAS appeal. https://www.communities-ni.gov.uk/articles/ni-pip-handbook-disputes-process and https://www.communities-ni.gov.uk/articles/ni-pip-handbook-how-make-claim
- Social Security Scotland public information: Adult Disability Payment route, daily living/mobility and work/income/savings statement. https://www.mygov.scot/adult-disability-payment

The official administrative/assessment sources control rules; Citizens Advice adds claimant-facing explanation. Medical evidence sources do not establish benefits law. No arbitrary third-party article or forum was used to decide deadlines or descriptors.

## Removed unsupported material

No diagnosis-specific tribunal success rate, no entitlement guarantee, no guaranteed representation, no phone exclusion, no worst-day-only instruction, no predetermined distance or cooking-time threshold, no generic condition-management filler, and no claim that physical transport access alone belongs to planning/following journeys. The old statement that appeals cannot affect other benefits and automatic backdating promises were removed, not replaced by a different unsupported blanket assertion. New claim versus challenge is framed as an individual-advice decision rather than a shortcut around deadlines.

- Citizens Advice mandatory reconsideration guide: explicit existing-award reduction/stoppage risk, not a blanket no-risk assertion. https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/benefits/sick-or-disabled-people-and-carers/pip/appeals/mandatory-reconsideration/
