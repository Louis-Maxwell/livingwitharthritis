# Anticholinergic burden source ledger, 19 November 2026

## Scope and limitations
Existing public article selected for medicine and triage risk, not a claimed traffic rank. Replaces thin generic advice, especially the unsafe routine GP/111 route for sudden new confusion. Preserves publication date, route and author. Removes inherited self-review fields and explicitly awaits a separate qualified clinical review. No dose changes, drug substitution or DIY taper schedule. A burden score is not a diagnosis or dementia prediction. NICE NG97 recommendations concern relevant dementia assessments/reviews, not a universal instruction to stop medicines.
