# 07 — Content strategy

**Corpus today:** ~**505** blog/library articles (public homepage claim). This is **volume**, not 200 finished cornerstones. Strategy: **curate → upgrade → fill gaps → prune**.

**Tone:** UK English; educational not diagnostic; NICE/NHS-aligned where clinical.

---

## Operating model

1. **Inventory** Champions + hubs; score depth, citations, a11y, internal links.  
2. **Upgrade** highest-traffic / highest-intent URLs into cornerstones (2,000–4,000+ words where warranted, structured, cited).  
3. **Fill** missing cluster spokes with intentional briefs — not AI spam.  
4. **Prune / noindex** thin city or duplicate intents that recreate soft-404 risk.  
5. **Target:** progress toward **~200 cornerstone-quality** pieces over a multi-year horizon; **12-month stretch:** **40–60** cornerstones + strong hubs — do **not** claim 200 already exist.

## What not to publish

- Diagnostic quizzes that output a disease label as fact.  
- Supplement cure claims.  
- Fake patient stories.  
- Thin city pages without unique value.
