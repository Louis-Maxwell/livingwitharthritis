## 1. Where in the UK do people search most for arthritis info?

### Findings (sourced)

- **Largest absolute joint-replacement search demand is in Greater London (related MSK intent).** Elanic Medical analysed Google data (Dec 2024–Nov 2025): UK average **~370,440 searches/month** for knee/hip replacement terms (**~255,770** knee; **~114,670** hip). Greater London led both: **~47,310/month** knee and **~25,080/month** hip. Source: [East London & West Essex Guardian / Elanic](https://www.guardian-series.co.uk/news/25880756.london-searches-knee-hip-replacements/).
- **Raw search volume ≠ per-capita need.** London’s population drives absolute volume; Fingertips GP Patient Survey shows London’s **self-reported long-term MSK prevalence is below England** (**13.4%** in 2023 vs England **18.4%**). Source: [OHID Fingertips – London MSK](https://fingertips.phe.org.uk/static-reports/health-trends-in-england/london/musculoskeletal_health.html); [GOV.UK MSK commentary Jan 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/musculoskeletal-health-local-profiles-january-2024-update/musculoskeletal-health-local-profiles-short-commentary-january-2024).
- **Highest regional prevalence (proxy for local info need) is the North East.** North East self-reported long-term MSK (“arthritis or ongoing problem with back or joints”) was **23.1%** in 2023 vs England **18.4%**. RA register prevalence also higher (**0.90%** NE vs **0.79%** England, FY ending 2025). Sources: [Fingertips North East](https://fingertips.phe.org.uk/static-reports/health-trends-in-england/North_East/musculoskeletal_health.html); [Fingertips England MSK](https://fingertips.phe.org.uk/static-reports/health-trends-in-england/england/musculoskeletal_health.html); HEEC brief: [HEEC](https://www.heec.co.uk/resource/what-works-health-and-care-interventions-to-support-people-from-disadvantaged-backgrounds-with-musculoskeletal-conditions/).
- **Other English regions above/near England average (2023 GPPS):** South West **19.0%**; North West ~**19.1%** (Fingertips regional pages). London remains the clear low-prevalence outlier among large regions. Source: [Fingertips South West](https://fingertips.phe.org.uk/static-reports/health-trends-in-england/South_West/musculoskeletal_health.html).
- **Deprived areas concentrate MSK burden (and therefore likely local search/support need).** OHID: most deprived decile more likely to report long-term MSK and MSK + another LTC (2023 and 2024 GPPS updates). Arthritis UK State of MSK Health 2025: higher hip/knee OA in most deprived 10% of England (e.g. knee OA **19.5%** most deprived vs **16.9%** least; severe knee OA **7%** vs **5.2%**). Sources: [GOV.UK April 2025 MSK commentary](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/musculoskeletal-health-profile-april-2025-update/musculoskeletal-health-profile-statistical-commentary-april-2025); [Arthritis UK State of MSK Health 2025 PDF](https://www.arthritis-uk.org/media/flpbvm2m/arthritisuk_state_of_msk_health_-report_2025.pdf).
- **England dominates absolute case counts** (so national SEO still skews English traffic): e.g. knee OA estimates **~4.62M England** vs **~432k Scotland**, **~274k Wales**, **~152k NI** (Arthritis UK / MSK Calculator figures in State of MSK 2025). Source: same PDF.
- **UK charity competitor traffic is UK-heavy.** Semrush public overview for `arthritis-uk.org` (Jul 2026): **65.54%** of visits from United Kingdom (**~178.4k** of **~272.2k** monthly visits); organic Google is **42.5%** of traffic. Source: [Semrush arthritis-uk.org overview](https://www.semrush.com/website/arthritis-uk.org/overview/).
- **UK Google Trends research exists for inflammatory arthritis co-queries, not a published city league table for “arthritis”.** Rheumatology observational study used Google Trends 2011–2022 for UK/USA IA terms (pain dominant). Source: [Rheumatology keae163.087 / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/keae163.087).

## 2. What related topics do people search for alongside arthritis?

### Findings (sourced)

- **Pain is the dominant accompanying search theme for inflammatory arthritis in the UK.** Google Trends (2011–2022): UK mean RSV for “pain” with RA **58**, PsA **34**, AS **39** — far above other symptom domains (stiffness, fatigue, mood, work typically **2–7** where available). Pain popularity rose over time. Source: [Rheumatology abstract P045](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/keae163.087).
- **Treatment / “treat-to-target” co-searches:** methotrexate was the leading T2T term with RA and PsA in the UK (mean RSV **28** and **21**); for AS, “inflammation” led (mean **9**). Still secondary to pain. Same source.
- **Semrush organic keywords driving Arthritis UK (UK database, Jul 2026) show medicine + supplement adjacency:** `arthritis` — position **2**, volume **74,000**, CPC **$0.44**; `naproxen` — position **4**, volume **165,000**, CPC **$0.82**; `glucosamine` — position **1**, volume **27,100**, CPC **$0.77**; brand/nav: `versus arthritis` **8,100**; `arthritis uk` **6,600**. Source: [Semrush arthritis-uk.org overview](https://www.semrush.com/website/arthritis-uk.org/overview/).
- **Charity information architecture mirrors high-demand self-management clusters:** Arthritis UK “Managing arthritis symptoms” hubs cover **pain, stiffness, fatigue, joint care/protection, common aches (neck/back/shoulder/knee), vaccinations, warmer weather, Mediterranean-style diet, mental health, stress**. Source: [arthritis-uk.org managing symptoms](https://www.arthritis-uk.org/information-and-support/understanding-arthritis/managing-arthritis-symptoms/).
- **NHS pathway language people are steered toward:** physiotherapy, occupational therapy, community **MSK self-referral** (back/knee/shoulder/neck pain, arthritis, sciatica, etc.), medicines, TENS, aids, surgery when severe. Sources: [NHS Arthritis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/arthritis/); [NHS community MSK self-referral](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/get-nhs-help-for-back-joint-problems/); [NHS Physiotherapy](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/physiotherapy/).
- **Flare / pacing / rest-vs-move** is a major UK clinical education theme (leaflets from NHS Grampian, Worcestershire Acute, Royal Berkshire, etc.) — strong long-tail content opportunity adjacent to “managing arthritis pain”. Example: [NHS Grampian flare leaflet](https://www.nhsgrampian.org/globalassets/foidocument/foi-public-documents1---all-documents/flare-up-advice-for-people-with-ia-leaflet.pdf).
- **Comorbidity / life-impact themes with published UK demand signals:** chronic pain (MSK cited as top longstanding condition among adults with chronic pain — **39%**, Health Survey for England 2024); mental health odds ratio **1.5** with MSK (OHID 2024); work/economic inactivity (Arthritis UK State of MSK cites ONS analyses). Sources: [NHS Digital chronic pain HSE 2024](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/health-survey-for-england/2024/chronic-pain); [GOV.UK April 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/musculoskeletal-health-profile-april-2025-update/musculoskeletal-health-profile-statistical-commentary-april-2025); [State of MSK 2025 PDF](https://www.arthritis-uk.org/media/flpbvm2m/arthritisuk_state_of_msk_health_-report_2025.pdf).
- **Seasonality (global RSV, not UK-only):** Infodemiology study of Google Trends 2004–2022 found significant seasonal patterns for arthritis / OA / RA / PsA / JIA with peaks late winter–early spring. Source: [PubMed 41168555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41168555/).

## 3. What other sites appear for “managing arthritis pain” searches?

### Findings (sourced — competitive set observed in UK-relevant results)

*Note: Without Semrush SERP API units we could not dump a live ranked top-10 for the exact phrase in `google.co.uk`. Below is the **verified competitive set** that consistently surfaces for this intent in UK search/indexing and is linked from NHS/NICE pathways.*

- **Arthritis UK (`arthritis-uk.org`, formerly Versus Arthritis)** — dedicated page *Managing arthritis pain* (medicines classes, self-help, TENS, pacing, reviewing meds). Strong UK charity authority. URL: https://www.arthritis-uk.org/information-and-support/understanding-arthritis/managing-arthritis-symptoms/managing-arthritis-pain/  
  Parent hub: https://www.arthritis-uk.org/information-and-support/understanding-arthritis/managing-arthritis-symptoms/
- **NHS.uk** — condition hubs (*Arthritis*, *Osteoarthritis – Treatment*, *Rheumatoid arthritis – Treatment / Living with*) covering physio, OT, painkillers/NSAIDs, steroids, DMARDs, heat/cold, aids, surgery, MSK self-referral. Examples: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/arthritis/ · https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/osteoarthritis/treatment/ · https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/rheumatoid-arthritis/living-with/
- **NRAS (`nras.org.uk`)** — *Managing the pain of rheumatoid arthritis* (non-drug: splints, heat/cold, strengthening, hydrotherapy; drug and surgical pathways). https://nras.org.uk/resource/managing-the-pain-of-rheumatoid-arthritis/
- **Patient.info** — UK consumer clinical encyclopaedia OA/arthritis pages (exercise, topical NSAIDs, weight, surgery). https://patient.info/bones-joints-muscles/arthritis/osteoarthritis
- **NICE** — OA quality standards / chronic pain guideline NG193 referenced in equity and pathway literature; informs NHS content authority. Entry points via https://www.nice.org.uk/
- **US / international publishers that still compete in UK SERPs:** Healthline (*Managing Arthritis Pain*), Mayo Clinic arthritis treatment, WebMD RA living-with content, Arthritis Foundation (`arthritis.org`). Healthline: https://www.healthline.com/health/chronic-pain/managing-arthritis-pain · Mayo: https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/arthritis/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20350777  
  Semrush: `arthritis.org` draws large global visits (Jul 2026 overview). https://www.semrush.com/website/arthritis.org/overview/
- **Local NHS trust / ICB patient leaflets** rank for long-tails (flare management, joint protection, pacing) — e.g. York Hospitals joint protection PDF, Worcestershire / Grampian / Royal Berkshire flare & pacing PDFs.
- **Semrush audience adjacency for Arthritis UK:** public overview lists `arthritis.org` and `hopkinsarthritis.org` among sites its audience also visits — signal of cross-border charity/academic competition. Source: [Semrush arthritis-uk.org](https://www.semrush.com/website/arthritis-uk.org/overview/).

## Suggested on-site content blocks (publish-ready headings + short copy)

### A. Hub: Managing arthritis pain (UK)

**H1: Managing arthritis pain day to day**  
Short copy: Pain is the symptom people look up most with arthritis. There is no single fix, but many people combine movement, pacing, heat or cold, prescribed or pharmacy medicines, and NHS therapies such as physiotherapy. This guide explains practical options used in the UK and when to speak to a GP or MSK service.

**H2: Self-help that people try first**  
Short copy: Stay as active as you safely can — complete rest can worsen stiffness. Try short, regular movement, heat for stiff joints, cold for hot swollen joints, and paced activity so good days do not become boom-and-bust.

**H2: Medicines — what to discuss with a pharmacist or GP**  
Short copy: Pain relief ranges from paracetamol and topical gels to NSAIDs such as naproxen, and for some people other prescribed options. Always check interactions and long-term risks; review medicines regularly.

**H2: NHS help without waiting for a diagnosis**  
Short copy: In many areas you can self-refer to community musculoskeletal (MSK) services for joint and back problems, including arthritis symptoms. Search “community MSK self-referral” plus your local area, or ask your GP surgery about First Contact Physiotherapy.

**H2: When to seek urgent care**  
Short copy: Seek urgent GP/NHS 111 advice for sudden severe pain and swelling in one joint, or pain that does not settle with simple painkillers. (Align wording to current NHS red-flag copy.)

### B. Regional / inequalities block

**H2: Arthritis support where need is highest**  
Short copy: Long-term joint and back problems are more common in some parts of England — for example the North East reports higher rates than the England average — and in more deprived neighbourhoods. If you live in an area with long waits, local MSK self-referral, community exercise, and peer support can help while you wait for specialist care.

### C. Related-topic cluster pages

1. **Exercising with arthritis (low-impact starters)**  
2. **Arthritis flare-ups: pacing, heat/cold, and when to rest**  
3. **Fatigue and arthritis: energy, sleep, and mental health**  
4. **Joint protection at home and work**  
5. **Diet and weight: what the evidence actually says**  
6. **Arthritis, work and fit notes — knowing your rights**  
7. **Medicines people search about: naproxen, NSAIDs, and supplements like glucosamine** (clear non-promotional, “ask a pharmacist” framing)

### D. SERP-competitive differentiator block

**H2: How we differ from NHS and big charities**  
Short copy: We are Living With Arthritis — a UK charity focused on everyday life with arthritis. Use NHS pages for clinical pathways, Arthritis UK for extensive condition libraries, and our site for peer-informed living tips, community, and navigating support.
