## Licence checks

The cat source releases its photo into the public domain worldwide. The dog and alpaca sources use CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . The horse source uses CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Horse attribution retains the supplied title, creator, source and licence link, and discloses conversion. Its source date is internally inconsistent, so no date is adopted.

These replace unrelated walking/community photographs whose alt text and captions described specific animals or veterinary activities absent from the images. The article advice and medical claims are unchanged by this repair and remain in the wider editorial audit.
