## What Louis must click / complete

1. Confirm Charity Commission listing is current (1218461).
2. Create / sign in to **Google for Nonprofits** with `info@livingwitharthritis.org.uk` (or Louis’s verified charity admin email).
3. Complete nonprofit verification for the UK CIO.
4. Accept Google for Nonprofits terms; enable **Ad Grants**.
5. Create a dedicated Google Ads account linked to Ad Grants (do not mix with paid Ads if any).
6. Submit Ad Grants application and wait for approval email.
7. After approval: build keyword groups from educational champions only (pain, OA, exercises, PIP, flare, newly diagnosed) — **no** misleading medical claims; land on HTTPS policy-compliant pages.
