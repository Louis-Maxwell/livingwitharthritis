# How we spend AI credits (plain English)

**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Audience:** CEO (no code)

## 1. What you asked for

You want a high-end Living With Arthritis site people can visit, read blogs, and donate from. You want the look and the jobs (help first, donate second) to stay strong. You also asked to improve how Google and AI assistants find and quote us (search, answer engines, generative engines).

You asked to **use Claude credits, not Lovable credits**, and to pick cheaper models when a cheaper one will do.

## 2. The plan in a few sentences

We already have a live charity site at **https://livingwitharthritis.org.uk**. We will not rebuild it from scratch, and we will not turn on a second paid AI product. Cursor/Claude edits the pages you can see: home, guides, blogs, donate. After each piece we check it ourselves (including a phone-sized layout) before we ask you to look.

We will **not** add Google login or a member database. Those were deliberately switched off. Admin sign-in still shows a paused message. Anyone can still read blogs, use the guides, and donate without an account.

We will **not** invent a new Netlify link. Share the real site: https://livingwitharthritis.org.uk

## 3. Which model to use (less tokens)

Think of models like staff grades. Do not send the most expensive person to change a headline.

- **Small jobs** (a title, a button, a FAQ link): cheapest capable model.
- **A whole page plus a check that it still works**: mid-range.
- **Many pages at once**: still mid-range; only step up if the mid-range model is clearly stuck.
- **Never** start a job on the most expensive setting.

One job, one pass. Do not re-read the whole website for a one-line change.

## 4. What visitors should see after this pass

- The browser tab on the home page matches the title search engines already see.
- A short UK line under the hero: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland — no town address on the home page.
- “Start here” includes money and PIP, not only exercises.
- Home-page questions are not dead ends: each answer has a page you can open (diet, help chat, osteoarthritis, PIP, waiting lists, work rights).
- Search engines and AI crawlers still get one UK “this is our website” card, with a short speakable intro they can quote.

## 5. What we will not do (unless you later decide)

- Spend Lovable AI credits.
- Put Google login or personal accounts back on.
- Publish a made-up Netlify preview as “the live site”.
- Put an Oswestry (or any) street address on the home page.
- Make donate louder than pain relief, newly diagnosed, and PIP.

## 6. Open decisions (no interview this run)

This run was started automatically on a pull request. There was no live Q&A and no screenshot attached. Until you click otherwise, we assume:

1. Keep the current look (red charity site, help first, donate quieter).
2. Keep blogs and donate public — no login wall.
3. Keep the live URL as livingwitharthritis.org.uk.
4. Keep admin / sign-in paused.
5. Keep UK-wide (four nations), not a local-only directory on the home page.

If any of those is wrong, say so in a follow-up and we will change only that.
