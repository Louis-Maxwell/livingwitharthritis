# Living With Arthritis

## Recognisable identity

Use **Living With Arthritis** as the public name. Keep the existing raised-arms
movement symbol, primary red, white space and dark text. Do not copy another
charity's identity or imply a partnership.

**Tagline:** Motion is Lotion

**Promise:** Practical support. Everyday confidence.

**Homepage headline:** Live well with arthritis, one step at a time.

**Three themes:** Move at your pace. Understand your options. Feel supported.

The reusable strings live in `src/config/brand.ts`. Keep the initial homepage
shell in `index.html` in sync with the visible hero; keep its meta description
in sync with the shared description.

## Voice and visual repetition

Use calm, empathetic British English and concrete next steps. Encourage people
without promising recovery, pain reduction or guaranteed outcomes. Distinguish
educational information from individual care, and show each guide's real review
status. Use the primary red for the mark and key actions, rather than adding
different colours for every condition. Keep help more prominent than donations.

For future social artwork, repeat the same mark, name and promise, with one
clear subject per poster. Use relevant movement or everyday-life imagery;
never imply that a stock-photo subject is a patient or testimonial. Do not
replace a page's specific title with a repeated slogan.

## Search visibility

Brand consistency supports recognition; it is not a guaranteed ranking change.
Keep the established brand-first homepage title, canonical URLs and verified
social identity links. Use unique, helpful titles on individual guides and
link readers to relevant exercise, pain, benefits and support resources.
Measure branded searches, impressions, clicks and CTR in Search Console after
publication, rather than treating a branding change as an immediate result.
