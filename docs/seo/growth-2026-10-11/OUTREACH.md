# Editorial partnerships and natural backlink acquisition

## Prospect qualification

The CSV contains 100 distinct organisation domains. Authority is a qualitative assessment, not invented Ahrefs DR or Moz DA. Five priority homepages were retrieved during this task; the remaining targets and every specific editorial opportunity still require verification. A live organisation homepage is not evidence of a link-placement opportunity.

First verify a relevant current page and contact: patient education, MSK/rheumatology, physiotherapy, accessibility, public involvement or an appropriate health editor. Read the contribution policy and check whether the charity's resource adds something useful beyond the organisation's own material. Record the exact source page, contact identity, asset, response and resulting link. Reject irrelevant, pay-for-ranking, automated posting or guaranteed-placement offers.

For NHS and university organisations, lead with usefulness and a professional relationship. Do not imply endorsement, use their logos without permission or ask for a link solely because their domain is strong. If they choose to cite a useful resource, accept their editorial decision and link attributes.

## Roadmap toward 100+ editorial links

| Period | Work | Useful milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Review two useful assets; verify 20 P1 prospects and contacts | Assets pass clinical/accessibility review; exact relevant pages recorded |
| Days 15–30 | Make ten personalised resource/partnership approaches | Track actual replies and feedback; improve assets |
| Days 31–60 | Expand relevant NHS, charity and physio relationships; pitch one original story | Report verified placements, referrals and editorial feedback |
| Days 61–90 | Develop a university/public-involvement project and a second PR asset | Document actual collaboration and publish transparent methods |
| Months 4–6 | Extend qualified prospects to about 250; continue selective outreach | Measure accepted opportunities and referring-domain diversity |
| Months 7–12+ | Extend to about 500 qualified prospects and repeat useful campaigns | Work toward 100 distinct editorial mentions/links; report outcomes honestly |

The 100-link objective is aspirational. For planning only, a 20% success rate across 500 suitable approaches would equal 100 placements; neither that success rate nor timing is established. Some accepted opportunities will yield no link, a nofollow link or a link from an already represented domain. NHS and university acceptance rates may be much lower. Evaluate referral value and reputation, not only link counts.

No purchased followed links, automated directory blasts, forced reciprocal links, spam comments, mass group posting, private blog networks, false affiliation or charity-endorsement claims are part of this plan.
